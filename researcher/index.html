Calculation and methodology notes

Theme and question averages use accepted responses in the current filter on the survey’s 1–7 scale.

n is the count of accepted rating sets (or answers) included in a figure. Cells with n < 5 are visually flagged as small-n.

Sample SD is used consistently for dispersion (including “most divided”). It describes spread around the mean; it is not a population claim.

Polarization (share at scale edges 1 and 7) may appear in detail layers but is not the ranking measure for most divided.

Segment comparisons are descriptive side-by-side summaries only — no significance tests.

None of these figures test causation, quality, or population-level claims.
