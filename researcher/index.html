Calculation and methodology notes

Theme and question averages use accepted responses in the current filter on the survey’s 1–7 scale.

n is the count of accepted rating sets (or answers) included in a figure.

Sample SD describes how spread out answers are around the mean.

Polarization is the share of answers at the scale edges (1 and 7).

Plain-English theme labels treat scores within 0.15 points as similar so tiny gaps are not overstated.

None of these figures test significance, causation, quality, or population-level claims.
