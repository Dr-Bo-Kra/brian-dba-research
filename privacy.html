DBA research · Participant information

Privacy and participant information

Notice version: 9 September 2026 · Research proposal: DBA 2027-10384

Scope change: This version updates the live research instrument from mixed-methods (ratings plus free-text reflections) to a quantitative-only instrument (coded demographics, vignette acknowledgement, and rating-scale items). Historical free-text, if previously stored, is not collected under this notice.

Protected research collection is currently disabled.

The public site remains a browser-only demonstration until the researcher enables protected collection. This notice describes how participation is intended to work under the approved study proposal. It is not a claim of GDPR, ISO, or other certification compliance.

1. Who is responsible

This activity is led by Brian E Pereira as part of a DBA research proposal titled Exploring Organizational Adoption of Alternative Creditworthiness Models for Inclusive Lending (proposal reference DBA 2027-10384). That proposal is the study basis. An institutional ethics-approval reference is not claimed here unless and until an approved ethics reference is recorded in this notice.

Brian is the research contact and is expected to be the sole active researcher for live stewardship. Contact him through the published LinkedIn profile. A Production institutional email address has not been published in this repository; do not invent one. Sponsoring-university controller or joint-controller details may be added here when AIM or the institution supplies them.

2. Purpose of the research

The study explores how lending professionals understand and assess psychometric, social-capital, behavioural-economic, organisational-readiness, fairness, and governance factors in responsible inclusive lending. Responses are for academic research and aggregate analysis. They are not used to make credit, employment, insurance, clinical, or other decisions about participants.

3. Who may participate

Participants must be at least 18 years old and take part in a professional capacity connected with lending, credit assessment, financial services, regulation, or a related field. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any point before submission without giving a reason. There is no artificial geographic blocking of the public site; eligibility is about professional capacity, not a claim that every local privacy law is met worldwide.

4. Information collected

  • Broad demographic and professional categories, including age band, gender response, education, institution type, position category, experience bands, involvement in lending decisions, and broad operating region.
  • Responses to a standardised lending vignette and rating-scale questions (quantitative instrument only).
  • A random participant reference, notice version, and consent timestamp.

The live study does not collect free-text reflections or open-ended role descriptions. The research payload does not intentionally include your name, email address, browser user-agent string, IP address, or page URL. Hosting and network providers may nevertheless process ordinary connection logs under their own operational policies. Responses are not completely anonymous while a participant reference remains linked to a record.

5. Please do not provide identifiable or confidential information

Do not include names, employer names, borrower or customer details, account information, case identifiers, unpublished commercial information, or special-category information in any communication about your participation (for example when requesting withdrawal). If such information was entered accidentally under an earlier notice that allowed free-text fields, contact Brian promptly and provide the participant reference from your downloaded record.

6. Lawful basis and research consent

The consent screen records your voluntary agreement to participate and confirms that you read this notice. The notice version and consent time are stored with any protected submission. Research-participation consent is distinct from any institutional legal basis AIM may later document. This notice does not claim that every applicable privacy statute has been certified.

7. Storage and retention

Browser-only demonstration

A completed record is kept in session storage for the current browser tab so that you can view and download your result. It is removed when the tab session ends, after a successful protected submission, or when you select “Delete local data and restart.” The site also deletes records left by older versions of the application.

Research archive

When protected collection is enabled, responses are stored in a private research database hosted in Singapore (approved hosting region for this study). Retention is 12 months after research completion, unless AIM requires a different period. Records that reach that threshold are surfaced for authorised researcher review. They are not automatically destroyed. Irreversible anonymisation or deletion happens only after an authorised review decision.

8. Recipients, processors, and international transfers

The public static site may be served from GitHub Pages or the approved research host. The research archive uses a protected submission endpoint and a private database in Singapore after collection is enabled. Authorised researchers review completed results in the authenticated inquiry archive. Access is role-based. The public website has no results list, no anonymous database read, and no researcher login. No advertising or behavioural analytics are intentionally used, and fonts are served locally by the participant’s operating system.

9. Security safeguards

The site minimises direct identifiers, uses a restrictive browser content policy, keeps collection disabled by default until deliberately enabled, removes long-lived browser archives, and requires any submission endpoint to validate and limit requests before inserting them into a row-level-security protected database. Researcher access uses individual accounts, multifactor authentication, least privilege, audited exports, and controlled deletion. No system can guarantee absolute security; these controls reduce risk.

10. Withdrawal and data rights

Before submission, close the activity or delete local data. After a protected submission, contact Brian (via the LinkedIn profile above), supply your participant reference from the downloaded record, and ask to withdraw. An authorised researcher will locate the record by that reference, confirm the request deliberately, and process deletion through the controlled, authenticated, audited researcher workflow—while the reference is still linked to the response and no legal hold applies. After irreversible anonymisation and inclusion in aggregate analysis, it may no longer be possible to identify and remove an individual record.

Institutional access, correction, erasure, or complaint routes may be added when AIM names the controller, privacy contact, and applicable authority. Until then, use Brian as the research contact for withdrawal requests.

11. Changes to this notice

The notice version is stored with each protected submission. Material changes require a new version and renewed participant acknowledgement. The researcher will not silently apply a later notice to previously collected data. Version 2026-09-09 records the approved change to a quantitative-only live instrument.
