DBA research · Participant information

Privacy and participant information

Notice version: 28 August 2026 · Research proposal: DBA 2027-10384

Protected research collection is currently disabled.

The public site operates as a browser-only demonstration until institutional ethics approval, controller details, the database region, processor agreements, and a protected submission endpoint are confirmed.

1. Who is responsible

This activity is led by Brian E Pereira as part of a DBA research proposal titled Exploring Organizational Adoption of Alternative Creditworthiness Models for Inclusive Lending. Brian is the current research contact. Contact him through the published LinkedIn profile.

The sponsoring university and final data-controller or joint-controller details must be inserted here before server-side research collection is enabled. The institution’s privacy contact, ethics committee reference, and supervisory-authority details must also be added after approval.

2. Purpose of the research

The study explores how lending professionals understand and assess psychometric, social-capital, behavioural-economic, organisational-readiness, fairness, and governance factors in responsible inclusive lending. Responses are for academic research and aggregate analysis. They are not used to make credit, employment, insurance, clinical, or other decisions about participants.

3. Who may participate

Participants must be at least 18 years old and take part in a professional capacity connected with lending, credit assessment, financial services, regulation, or a related field. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any point before submission without giving a reason.

4. Information collected

  • Broad demographic and professional categories, including age band, gender response, education, institution type, position category, experience bands, involvement in lending decisions, and broad operating region.
  • Responses to a standardised lending vignette and rating-scale questions.
  • Free-text reflections about organisational adoption, ethics, governance, regulation, and inclusive lending.
  • A random participant reference, notice version, and consent timestamp.

The research payload does not intentionally include your name, email address, browser user-agent string, IP address, or page URL. Hosting and network providers may nevertheless process ordinary connection logs independently under their own operational policies.

5. Please do not provide identifiable or confidential information

Do not type names, employer names, borrower or customer details, account information, case identifiers, unpublished commercial information, or special-category information into free-text fields. If such information is entered accidentally, contact the researcher promptly and provide the participant reference from your downloaded record.

6. Lawful basis and research consent

The consent screen records your voluntary agreement to participate and confirms that you read this notice. Research-participation consent is distinct from the legal basis used by the final sponsoring institution to process personal data. The institution must document the appropriate legal basis for each applicable jurisdiction before collection is enabled.

7. Storage and retention

Browser-only demonstration

A completed record is kept in session storage for the current browser tab so that you can view and download your result. It is removed when the tab session ends, after a successful protected submission, or when you select “Delete local data and restart.” The site also deletes records left by older versions of the application.

Research archive

When protected collection is eventually enabled, the approved retention period, database region, backup period, deletion schedule, and anonymisation point must be stated here. Until those items are approved, server-side collection remains disabled.

8. Recipients, processors, and international transfers

The public static site is hosted using GitHub Pages. A future research archive may use a protected submission endpoint and a private database only after the sponsoring institution approves the processor agreement, hosting region, access controls, and international-transfer mechanism.

If collection is later enabled, authorised researchers would review completed results in the authenticated database dashboard. Access is role-based. The public website has no results list, no anonymous database read, and no researcher login. No advertising or behavioural analytics are intentionally used, and fonts are served locally by the participant’s operating system rather than requested from Google Fonts.

9. Security safeguards

The site minimises direct identifiers, uses a restrictive browser content policy, keeps collection disabled by default, removes long-lived browser archives, and requires any future submission endpoint to validate and limit requests before inserting them into a row-level-security protected database. Researcher access must use individual accounts, multifactor authentication, least privilege, and auditable exports.

10. Withdrawal and data rights

Before submission, close the activity or delete local data. After a protected submission, contact the researcher with the participant reference in your downloaded record. A request can be actioned while the reference is linked to the response. After irreversible anonymisation and inclusion in aggregate analysis, it may no longer be possible to identify and remove an individual record.

The final institutional notice must describe applicable access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, grievance, nomination, and complaint rights for each participant jurisdiction, together with response times and the responsible privacy authority.

11. Changes to this notice

The notice version is stored with each protected submission. Material changes require a new version and renewed participant acknowledgement. The researcher will not silently apply a later notice to previously collected data.
